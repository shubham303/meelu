# Your Own CTR Curve: Finding Titles That Underperform

**Topic suggestion:** Your Own CTR Curve: Finding Titles That Underperform

**Summary:** Build a site-specific CTR-by-position curve from non-branded queries, compute expected minus actual clicks (ctr_gap), and rank pages by gap × impressions. Then have an LLM suggest title and meta rewrites for the human to pick from.
