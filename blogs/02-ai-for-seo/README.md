# Part 2 — AI for SEO

The largest part of the book. SEO has rich data (crawl, Search Console, keyword vendors, SERPs) and clear levers (titles, content, links), so it is where AI helps most today. It is split into five sections.

[← Back](../README.md)

## Sections

1. [Technical SEO](01-technical-seo/README.md) — Rules find the problems deterministically, and AI helps triage, explain and prioritise them.
2. [Search Console Intelligence](02-search-console-intelligence/README.md) — Search Console is the most honest SEO data you have. These chapters turn it into decisions.
3. [Keyword Intelligence](03-keyword-intelligence/README.md) — From a pile of queries to a map of topics, owners and gaps.
4. [Site Structure and Internal Linking](04-site-structure/README.md) — Internal links are the SEO lever you fully control. AI can find the links worth adding, the ones worth removing and the hub pages worth building.
5. [AI Search Visibility](05-ai-search-visibility/README.md) — Search is no longer only ten blue links. These chapters look at being found and cited by AI answer engines.
