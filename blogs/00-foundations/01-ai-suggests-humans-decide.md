# AI Suggests, Humans Decide: The Operating Principle for Marketing AI

**Topic suggestion:** AI Suggests, Humans Decide: The Operating Principle for Marketing AI

**Summary:** Open the book with the stance: marketing AI should end every automated step in a suggestion a person can approve, edit or reject, never a silent change to the site. Explain why (taste, brand judgement, accountability, LLM error rates) and what a good suggestion carries: the data, the pages involved and the reason. Contrast with 'autopilot SEO' tools that rewrite pages on their own.

**Approach:** Principle

**Grounded in Meelu:** The whole product. Every desk ends in reviewable rows with states such as draft, ready and user_rejected.
