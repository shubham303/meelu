# Grounding and Trust: Making AI Show Its Evidence

**Topic suggestion:** Grounding and Trust: Making AI Show Its Evidence

**Summary:** How to stop an AI from making up marketing numbers. Cover evidence fields (quotes plus URLs), trust levels and caveats on statistical results, the 'declined' pattern where the tool refuses rather than guesses, and date freshness checks. Frame it this way: a confident wrong number costs more than an honest 'the data is too thin'.

**Approach:** Both

**Grounded in Meelu:** Analytics results carry a trust block and a declined flag. Lead evidence quotes pages the pipeline actually read. Guides tell the agent to anchor windows to the last synced day.
