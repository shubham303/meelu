Free keyword research from a single page

Given a page, figure out what it is trying to sell or convey. From that intent, form the queries a buyer or reader would actually type. Run those queries as real web searches and look at what came back: the pages that rank are the pages the search engine believes answer the query.

Analyse those ranking pages. Extract the keywords and phrases they lean on — the terms they repeat, the questions they answer, the angles they take. Then check our own page against that list: which of those terms and questions does it already cover, and which is it missing?

The keywords that survive this comparison go into the keyword table, tied to the page they came from. No paid keyword tool involved — the search results themselves are the data source.

This is a workflow, not a tool. The chain (read page → infer intent → form queries → search → fetch and analyse results → diff against our page → save keywords) is one recipe among endlessly many; tomorrow's variant starts from a competitor's page, or from a query instead of a page, or diffs against a whole section. Workflows like this belong in the workflow section, composed from the small tools the agent already has (read a page, web search, fetch a URL, save keywords) — not baked into the desk as a single monolithic "research keywords" tool.
