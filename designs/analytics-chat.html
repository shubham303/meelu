Analytics

Ask questions of your data. A session is one body of files and one conversation about them — drop a CSV to start, add more files as you go, or ask Meelu to pull data it can already reach. Everything runs on your device; raw files never leave it.

Conversations aren't kept yet — when an answer is worth returning to, save it as a report. Reports live in your library for good, even if the session is deleted.
