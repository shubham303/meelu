## The short answer

Writesonic calls itself an AI search growth engine. It tracks how ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews answer the prompts you care about, ranks fixes in an Action Center on higher plans, writes long articles, and audits your site. It connects to Search Console, Ahrefs and many content management systems. Starter is described as for solo founders and first marketing hires, and Growth for marketing teams of 3 to 10.

Meelu is a desktop app for founders and marketers. It crawls your site, reads your Search Console and Analytics data, checks what ChatGPT, Claude and Google's AI Overview say for the questions you track, and tells you what to do next with the evidence. The same app writes pages for your site, finds social posts where people ask for a tool like yours, and finds people worth a message.

## Where Writesonic is better

- **Daily AI tracking at volume.** Every plan tracks dozens of prompts a day, with sentiment and prompt volumes on higher plans.
- **Fix and measure.** Its Action Center ranks fixes, and Writesonic measures each one after 14 and 28 days.
- **Integrations.** Ahrefs, Search Console, many content management systems and CDNs, a read-only MCP server and a REST API.

## Where Meelu is better

- **It checks your page, not just the answer.** For each question, Meelu marks which points your page answers, partly answers or misses, and links that to your Google ranking.
- **A bigger crawl for the price.** Up to 10,000 pages, plus Googlebot rendering checks on desktop and mobile that you start yourself.
- **People, not only rankings.** Social listening and lead finding are in the same app.
- **Price.** About $15 a month per website, with AI and data at what they cost.

## Pricing

Writesonic Starter is $99 a month, or $79 a month billed yearly. Basic is $249 a month ($199 yearly) and Growth $499 a month ($399 yearly). Enterprise is priced on request. There is a 7-day free trial with no card and a 7-day refund.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Crawling and importing Search Console data are free. Your first website is free for the first day.
