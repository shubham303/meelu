## The short answer

Ahrefs is known for its backlink and keyword data. Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit and Rank Tracker are tools for people who know SEO and want to dig. Brand Radar AI, its AI search product, is an add-on from $199 a month, and paid plans from Lite include some AI prompt checks.

Meelu is for a founder or a small team. It crawls your site, reads your Search Console and Analytics data, and tells you what is worth your time, with the evidence. Beyond SEO, it checks how ChatGPT, Claude and Google's AI Overview answer your customers' questions, writes pages for your site, finds social posts where people ask for a tool like yours, and finds leads.

## Where Ahrefs is better

- **Backlink data.** Ahrefs' index is one of the largest, and you can explore any domain.
- **A free tier for your own site.** Ahrefs Free gives a site audit and a limited view of your own keywords and backlinks.
- **Research freedom.** Look up any competitor, any keyword, any time, within your plan's limits.

## Where Meelu is better

- **No SEO expertise needed.** Meelu explains what it found in plain words and says why it matters.
- **AI search is in the same app.** GEO checks run on the same plan, charged at what each check costs.
- **It covers more than SEO.** Content writing, social listening, finding leads and data analytics are in the same app.
- **It suggests, you decide.** Meelu never changes your site without your approval. Each suggestion carries the pages and numbers behind it.

## Pricing

Ahrefs Starter is $29 a month for keyword and competitor research. Lite is $129 a month for 1 user, 5 projects for sites you have not verified (unlimited for verified sites), 750 tracked keywords and 150 AI prompt checks. Brand Radar AI starts at $199 a month on top of a plan.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Crawling and importing Search Console data are free. Your first website is free for the first day.
