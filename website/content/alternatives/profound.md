## The short answer

Profound is built for enterprise marketing, content and PR teams. It tracks up to nine answer engines, shows prompt volumes, and reports on AI crawler traffic to your site. You can try it free for 7 days, and its Enterprise plan is sold through a demo.

Meelu is built for a founder or a small SaaS team. Its GEO desk checks the questions your customers ask. For each one, it reads your closest pages, asks ChatGPT and Claude with web search on, reads Google's AI Overview, and tells you whether you are cited and what to do next. You can start today, and the price is on the website.

GEO is one part of Meelu. The same app does SEO, content writing, social listening, finding leads and data analytics.

## Where Profound is better

- **Breadth of AI engines.** Profound covers up to nine, including Copilot and DeepSeek.
- **Enterprise needs.** SSO, an API, custom prompts and daily tracking at scale.
- **AI crawler analytics.** It shows how AI bots visit your site, through integrations with Cloudflare, Vercel and others.

## Where Meelu is better

- **You can keep going without a sales call.** Download the app, add your site, and run your first check the same day. Your price is public and you pay only for what you use.
- **It explains the gap.** Meelu shows which points of the question your page answers and which it misses, quoting the passage it found.
- **It links AI search to Google rankings.** If your page answers the question but ranks below Google's first page, Meelu says so, because AI search rarely finds it.
- **The rest of your marketing is in the same app**, from technical SEO to Reddit threads where people ask for a tool like yours.

## Pricing

Profound has no public price for brands. Its pricing page offers an Enterprise plan through a demo and a free 7-day trial with 50 prompts a day across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Agencies can buy a self-serve Agency Growth plan.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that 0.25 credits a day, plus the AI answers and search data each check uses, at cost. Your first website is free for the first day.
