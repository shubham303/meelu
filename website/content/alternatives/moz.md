## The short answer

Moz Pro is one of the oldest SEO suites. It has keyword research, rank tracking, a site crawl, link research with its Domain Authority score, and AI prompt tracking that updates weekly. Plans start at $49 a month for one website.

Meelu is for a founder or a small team marketing their own SaaS. It reads your site and your Search Console and Analytics data, and tells you what to fix and what to write next, with the evidence. The same app checks AI answers, writes pages, finds social posts where people ask for a tool like yours, and finds leads.

## Where Moz is better

- **Domain Authority.** Many people use Moz's link score as a shared yardstick.
- **Rank tracking of a fixed list.** Moz checks Google for the keywords you pick, even before your site shows up for them in Search Console.
- **The MozBar** browser extension for quick checks while you browse.

## Where Meelu is better

- **It explains and suggests.** Meelu tells you what it found and why it matters, so you do not need to read every report.
- **It writes the page.** The Content desk drafts pages for the searches you can win, with links to your existing pages.
- **It finds people.** Social listening and lead finding are in the same app.
- **AI search checks explain the gap**, by showing which points of a question your page answers.

## Pricing

Moz Pro has an entry plan at $49 a month for one website. Standard is $99 a month, or $79 a month billed yearly. Extra AI prompts cost $50 per 100.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Your first website is free for the first day.
