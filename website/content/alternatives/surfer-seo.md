## The short answer

Surfer is best known for its content editor. You give it a keyword, it looks at the pages that rank for it, and it scores your draft against them. It also has keyword research, a Topical Map from your Search Console data, content audits and AI search tracking.

Meelu starts from your own site. It reads your pages and your Search Console data, finds the searches you could win, and writes the page for that search. Then it checks the draft and suggests internal links to pages you already have. Content is one part of Meelu. The same app also does technical SEO, GEO, social listening, finding leads and data analytics.

Pick Surfer if you write a lot of content and want a score for every draft. Pick Meelu if you are a founder or a small team and want one tool for your whole site.

## Where Surfer is better

- **A score for every draft.** Surfer compares your text with the pages that rank and tells you which terms to add. Meelu checks the page fields and the text, but it does not score against competitors.
- **Integrations for writers.** From the Standard plan, Surfer works inside Google Docs and WordPress. Meelu keeps drafts in the app, and can save a page as a draft in Webflow or Contentful once you connect them.
- **Many writers at once.** Surfer's plans include up to 360 documents a month, or unlimited on Peace of Mind, which suits a content team.

## Where Meelu is better

- **It reads your site.** Meelu crawls your pages and reads your Search Console data, so it can tell you which search to write for, which page already covers it, and what technical problems the site has.
- **It does more than content.** A page cannot rank if Google cannot read it. Meelu also finds the technical problems that stop that, such as content that loads only with JavaScript.
- **It finds people who need you now.** The Social Listening desk finds posts where people ask for a tool like yours. The Find Leads desk finds people worth a message.
- **You stay in control.** Meelu never publishes and never changes your live site. Every step ends in a suggestion you approve, edit or turn down.

## Pricing

Surfer's cheapest plan, Discovery, is $49 a month billed yearly. It includes 120 documents and tracking for 10 pages. AI search tracking starts on Standard, at $99 a month billed yearly.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls and keyword data are charged at what they cost, with no markup. Crawling your site and importing Search Console data are free. Your first website is free for the first day.
