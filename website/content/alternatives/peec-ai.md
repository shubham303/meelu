## The short answer

Peec AI is an analytics tool for AI search. It tracks how often AI models mention your brand, in what position, with what sentiment, and which sources they cite. It is built for marketing teams and agencies, with unlimited users on every plan.

Meelu's GEO desk asks a different question: does a page on your site answer this question, and if not, what should you do? For each question you track, Meelu reads your closest pages and marks every point a full answer needs as answered, partly answered or missing. It asks ChatGPT and Claude the same question with web search on, and reads Google's AI Overview. Then it gives a verdict and a suggestion.

GEO is one part of Meelu. The same app does SEO, content writing, social listening, finding leads and data analytics for your site.

## Where Peec AI is better

- **More AI models and more detail on your brand.** Peec AI tracks position and sentiment, and classifies the sources AI models cite.
- **Teams.** Every plan has unlimited users, and the higher plans connect to Looker Studio.
- **Daily tracking** of a set of prompts without you starting a check.

## Where Meelu is better

- **It checks your page against the question.** Meelu shows which points your page misses, or that you have no page at all.
- **It connects AI search to Google.** The best page's average position comes from your Search Console data. If a page answers fully but ranks below Google's first page, Meelu says so, because AI search rarely finds those pages.
- **It helps you fix it.** The Content desk writes the missing page, and the Site desk finds technical problems that hide your text from AI crawlers.
- **One tool for a small team.** You do not need a separate tracker, SEO suite and Reddit monitor.

## Pricing

Peec AI's Starter plan costs $95 a month, or $80 a month billed yearly. It has 50 prompts, 3 models of your choice, unlimited users and daily tracking. Pro costs $245 a month and Advanced $495 a month. Enterprise is priced on request. An extra model on Starter costs $35 a month.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. Each GEO check uses credits for the AI answers and search data, at what they cost. A website can track up to 100 questions. Your first website is free for the first day.
