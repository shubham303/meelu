## The short answer

Clearscope helps content teams write pages that cover a topic well. It suggests the terms to include, drafts and edits with AI, and tracks queries in Google, ChatGPT and Gemini. Its customers include large companies, and the price starts at $129 a month.

Meelu is for a founder or a small team doing their own marketing. It reads your site and your Search Console data, picks the search a new page should target, writes it with links to your existing pages, and checks it. Content is one part of the app. Meelu also does technical SEO, GEO, social listening, finding leads and data analytics.

## Where Clearscope is better

- **Term-level guidance.** Clearscope shows which terms the ranking pages use and grades your draft.
- **Content teams.** Large companies such as Adobe, Shopify and IBM use it, and it has content analytics for published pages.

## Where Meelu is better

- **Price for a small team.** One website costs about $15 a month, plus what the AI and data actually cost.
- **It knows what to write next.** Meelu finds openings in your own Search Console data and your crawled pages.
- **It covers the site, not only the draft.** The same app checks technical problems and AI crawler access, and the writer picks internal links from your crawled pages.
- **It finds people as well as searches.** Social listening and lead finding sit in the same app.

## Pricing

Clearscope Essentials is $129 a month. It has a 14-day free trial.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls and keyword data are charged at what they cost. Your first website is free for the first day.
