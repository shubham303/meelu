## The short answer

OpenSEO is an open source SEO tool, positioned as an alternative to Ahrefs and Semrush. It covers keyword research, domain overviews, backlinks, rank tracking, a site audit and AI visibility. It connects to Google Search Console. Its MCP server lets your own AI agent, such as Claude, Codex or ChatGPT, use that data. You can use the hosted version or self-host it for free.

Meelu is a desktop app for Mac and Windows with its own AI coworker. It crawls your site from your computer, reads your Search Console and Analytics data, and tells you what to fix and what to write next, with the evidence. SEO is one part of it. The same app checks AI search answers against your pages, writes pages for your site, finds social posts where people ask for a tool like yours, finds leads, and answers questions about your data with charts.

Pick OpenSEO if you want open source, already work in an AI agent like Claude Code, and mainly need SEO data. Pick Meelu if you want one app that covers your marketing, with suggestions you approve.

## Where OpenSEO is better

- **Open source.** You can read the code, fork it and host it yourself under the MIT license.
- **It works in the agent you already use.** If you live in Claude Code or Codex, OpenSEO brings SEO data there.
- **Low entry price.** The $10 plan includes $10 of usage, and Search Console data does not use credits.
- **Research on any domain**, with traffic estimates and ranking keywords.

## Where Meelu is better

- **More than SEO.** Content writing, social listening, finding leads and data analytics are part of the same app.
- **An agent built for this job.** You do not need to set up MCP or write prompts. Each desk has ready tasks, and Meelu explains each finding with the pages and numbers behind it.
- **AI search checks that explain the gap.** Meelu shows which points of a question your page answers, and links that to your Google ranking.
- **A crawl that sees what AI crawlers miss.** Meelu can render your pages as Googlebot on desktop and mobile and flags content that only appears after JavaScript runs.
- **Nothing changes without you.** Meelu never edits your site or publishes a page. It posts a social reply only after you approve it, to a thread you picked.

## Pricing

OpenSEO's hosted Base Plan is $10 a month and includes $10 of usage each month. Its pricing page estimates about $0.05 per keyword search, $0.08 per backlink check and $0.002 per AI prompt check. The free trial includes $0.50 of credits. Self-hosting is free, and you pay DataForSEO for the data.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Crawling and importing Search Console and Analytics data are free. Your first website is free for the first day.
