## The short answer

Okara calls itself an AI CMO. It researches your product, writes strategy documents, and runs agents for SEO, GEO, articles, Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Hacker News launch posts, UGC videos and influencer outreach. Its coding agent opens GitHub pull requests for SEO fixes. On the Pro plan it can publish articles to WordPress, Webflow and Framer, and post to X and LinkedIn. Reddit replies are copied and posted by you.

Meelu is a desktop app for founders and marketers that covers your organic marketing. It crawls your site, reads your Search Console and Analytics data, checks AI answers against your own pages, writes pages, finds conversations to join and finds people worth a message. Meelu never publishes or changes your site. Every step ends in a suggestion you approve, edit or turn down.

## Where Okara is better

- **More channels.** Articles, X, LinkedIn, Hacker News launch posts, UGC videos and influencers from one strategy.
- **Pull requests for fixes.** Its coding agent opens reviewable GitHub pull requests for SEO and GEO fixes.
- **A real free plan** with strategy documents and a home page audit.
- **Volume.** If you want 30 posts and articles a month written and published for you, Okara is built for that.

## Where Meelu is better

- **You stay in control.** Meelu never publishes, posts or changes your site on its own.
- **Leads.** Find Leads looks for people who show they need your product, with the reason and a link to reach them.
- **Wider listening.** Hacker News, Stack Exchange, Dev.to, GitHub, Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Facebook, as well as Reddit.
- **AI search checks that explain the gap**, point by point against your own page.
- **Price.** About $15 a month per website, with AI and data at what they cost.

## Pricing

Okara's free plan has 5 one-time credits. AI CMO Lite is $129 a month ($1,290 a year) and AI CMO Pro is $249 a month ($2,490 a year), each with 2,000 credits a month. Extra credits start at $45 for 860.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Crawling and importing Search Console data are free. Your first website is free for the first day.
