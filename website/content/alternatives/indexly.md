## The short answer

Indexly calls itself "AI Search Growth on Autopilot". It tracks how AI engines mention you, writes and refreshes articles, posts to LinkedIn, X and Instagram, monitors Reddit, and puts Search Console, AI referral traffic and social traffic in one dashboard. Content and social agents can run in review-first mode or on full autopilot. It is built for growth, marketing, content and demand-generation teams.

Meelu is a desktop app for founders and marketers. It crawls your site, reads your Search Console and Analytics data, checks AI answers against your own pages, drafts pages, listens for conversations on nine platforms and the open web and finds people worth a message. Meelu never publishes or posts on its own. Every step ends in a suggestion you approve.

## Where Indexly is better

- **More AI engines tracked daily**, with countries, sentiment and AI crawler logs.
- **Autopilot publishing** for articles and social posts, if that is what you want.
- **Pay per module.** Buy only SEO or only forum monitoring, and use Analytics for free.
- **Agency features**: white label, API, Zapier and webhooks.

## Where Meelu is better

- **Nothing goes live without you.** Meelu suggests, and you publish.
- **One plan.** SEO, AI search, content, social listening and leads come together.
- **Wider listening.** Hacker News, Stack Exchange, Dev.to, GitHub, Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Facebook, as well as Reddit.
- **Leads.** Find Leads looks for people who show they need your product.
- **A bigger crawl**, with checks for content that appears only after JavaScript runs.

## Pricing

Indexly sells modules: SEO Visibility $49 a month, AI Visibility $99, Content Agents $99, Social Agents $99, Forum Agents $49 and Analytics free. The Indexly One bundle starts at $299 a month. Its pricing page also shows Starter at $99, Growth at $299 and Scale at $499 a month.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Your first website is free for the first day.
