## The short answer

Ubersuggest, from Neil Patel, is a low-cost SEO suite. It has keyword suggestions, a site audit, rank tracking, backlinks, competitor analysis and AI search visibility. On the lowest plan, rankings and audits update weekly and AI prompts update monthly.

Meelu is for a founder or a small team doing their own marketing. It crawls your site, up to 10,000 pages, from your computer, and the crawl uses no credits beyond the website's daily fee, reads your Search Console and Analytics data, and tells you what to fix and what to write next, with the evidence. The same app checks AI answers, writes pages, finds social posts where people ask for a tool like yours, and finds leads.

## Where Ubersuggest is better

- **Fixed price.** You know the monthly cost up front, and lifetime plans are available.
- **Keyword ideas for any topic**, with no per-query cost, up to 150 reports a day on the lowest plan.
- **A web app** you can open anywhere.

## Where Meelu is better

- **Fresh data from your own site.** Meelu crawls about once a day while the app is open, and imports your Search Console and Analytics data.
- **It explains and suggests**, with the evidence for each suggestion, and you can ask follow-up questions.
- **More than SEO.** Content writing, social listening and lead finding are part of the same app.

## Pricing

Ubersuggest lists plans from $29 to $99 a month, with a 7-day trial for monthly plans.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Crawling and importing Search Console data are free. Your first website is free for the first day.
