## The short answer

Semrush is a large toolkit for marketers and agencies. It covers keyword research, competitor and backlink data, site audits, rank tracking, content tools and AI search tracking. Social monitoring and lead generation are separate paid apps. The SEO plan, which most people compare, starts at $139 a month.

Meelu is for a founder or a small team doing marketing for their own SaaS. Instead of a toolbox, it gives you an AI coworker that reads your site and your Search Console and Analytics data, points at what is worth your time, and explains why. It covers SEO, GEO, content writing, social listening, finding leads and data analytics in one desktop app. It suggests, and you decide.

Pick Semrush if you run an agency, research many competitors every day or buy ads. Pick Meelu if you want to know what to do next on your own site, and want people who need your product today.

## Where Semrush is better

- **Data depth.** Semrush keeps its own keyword and backlink database, with years of history across the whole web.
- **Competitor research at scale.** You can look up any domain, its traffic, keywords and ads, as often as you like within your plan.
- **Advertising, local and agency features**, including white-label reports.
- **A web app.** It works from any browser and supports teams.

## Where Meelu is better

- **It tells you what to do.** You do not need to know which report to open. Ask "what should I fix first?" and Meelu answers with the pages, the numbers and the reason.
- **It starts from your own data.** Meelu reads your Search Console data first, so it finds the searches you are close to winning.
- **Social listening and leads are in the same app.** Meelu finds posts on Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Hacker News and more where people ask for a tool like yours, and finds people worth a message.
- **You pay for what you use.** One website is about $15 a month. Keyword data and AI calls cost what the provider charges.
- **Nothing changes without you.** Meelu never edits your site or publishes a page. It posts a social reply only after you approve it, to a thread you picked. Every step ends in a suggestion.

## Pricing

Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed yearly, for 5 websites and 500 tracked keywords. The SEO and AI Search plan, Starter, is $199 a month. There is a free plan with 10 reports a day and a 7-day trial. Brand Monitoring is a separate app.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost, with no markup. Crawling your site and importing Search Console and Analytics data are free. Your first website is free for the first day.
