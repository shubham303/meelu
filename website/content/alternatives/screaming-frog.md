## The short answer

Screaming Frog SEO Spider is a widely used crawler for technical SEO experts. It runs on your computer, finds hundreds of kinds of issues, renders JavaScript, connects to Search Console and Analytics, and exports everything. It is built for people who want the raw data and full control over the crawl.

Meelu is a desktop app too, and it crawls your site from your computer, with no charge per crawl. The difference is what happens next. Meelu groups the issues into four levels, from Fix now to Polish, names the pages, and you can ask it in plain English what to fix first. It can also render your pages as Googlebot on desktop and mobile, and flags text, links or tags that only appear after JavaScript runs, which many AI crawlers never see.

The crawl is one part of Meelu. The same app does keyword research, AI search checks, content writing, social listening, finding leads and data analytics.

## Where Screaming Frog is better

- **Control.** Custom extraction, crawl configuration, scheduling and a command line.
- **Scale.** Crawl very large sites, limited mainly by your memory and disk.
- **Price for a pure crawler.** One licence covers any number of sites for a year, while Meelu charges per website.

## Where Meelu is better

- **You do not need to be an SEO expert.** Meelu tells you what is wrong, where, and what to do about it.
- **The crawl meets your search data.** Meelu has your Search Console clicks next to the crawl, so you can ask which problems sit on pages that get traffic.
- **It does the next step too.** Write the page, check if ChatGPT cites you, find the Reddit thread where someone asks for your product.

## Pricing

Screaming Frog's free version crawls up to 500 URLs. A licence is £199 a year per user.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. Crawling uses no credits beyond that fee. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Your first website is free for the first day.
