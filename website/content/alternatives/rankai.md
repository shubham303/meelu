## The short answer

RankAI is a Y Combinator-backed agent that "researches, publishes, and rewrites until your pages rank on Google and AI search". It finds queries, publishes articles to your CMS, watches Search Console, and rewrites its articles that are not in Google's top 50. Its onboarding recommends Auto Publish, and a review-first mode is available. Its blog integration edits only the articles it creates. It also audits your site, and its script can apply the technical fixes you switch on, such as titles, meta descriptions and alt text, to your existing pages. It tracks ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews about every 14 days.

Meelu is a desktop app for founders and marketers. It crawls your whole site, reads your Search Console and Analytics data, finds the searches you could win, drafts the page and checks it. You publish it. The same app checks AI answers against your pages, finds conversations to join and finds people worth a message.

## Where RankAI is better

- **Hands off.** If you want pages published and rewritten without touching them, RankAI does that.
- **It closes the loop.** It watches how its pages rank and rewrites them, and rewrites do not count toward your page allowance.
- **CMS coverage.** WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Framer, Wix, Squarespace, Sanity and an API for code sites.

## Where Meelu is better

- **You decide what ships.** Meelu never publishes or changes your site. Your brand and your judgement stay in charge.
- **Your whole site.** Meelu crawls up to 10,000 pages and suggests changes to the pages you already have, not only new ones. RankAI rewrites only the articles it creates.
- **Beyond SEO.** Social listening, lead finding and analytics questions are in the same app.
- **Price.** About $15 a month per website, with AI and data at what they cost.

## Pricing

RankAI's free plan does not publish. Starter is $199 a month for one website and 10 pages a month. Growth is $499 a month. Expert is custom. Yearly billing is 20% off.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Crawling and importing Search Console data are free. Your first website is free for the first day.
