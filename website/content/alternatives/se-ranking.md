## The short answer

SE Ranking is an SEO platform with rank tracking, a website audit, keyword, competitor and backlink research, a content editor, local SEO and AI visibility tracking. Its Core plan is $129 a month for 10 projects, and agencies can add white label.

Meelu is for a founder or a small team marketing their own SaaS. It reads your site and your Search Console and Analytics data, then tells you what to fix and what to write next, with the evidence. The same app checks AI answers, writes pages, finds social posts where people ask for a tool like yours, and finds leads.

## Where SE Ranking is better

- **Agency work.** Many projects, white label and client reports.
- **Daily rank tracking** for thousands of keywords, including local results.
- **A web app** with mobile apps, an API and an MCP server.

## Where Meelu is better

- **It tells you what to do next**, from your own data, with the evidence for each suggestion, and you can ask follow-up questions in plain English.
- **People, not only rankings.** Social listening and lead finding are in the same app, charged per run at cost.
- **Lower cost for one site**, with keyword data and AI calls at what they cost.

## Pricing

SE Ranking Core is $129 a month, with a 14-day free trial and no free plan. The AI Search toolkit add-on is $89 a month.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Your first website is free for the first day.
