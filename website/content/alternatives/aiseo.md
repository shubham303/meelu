## The short answer

aiseo.ai brings writing tools, AI visibility reports, Reddit tools and an SEO agent into one platform, sold as separate plans. Its Reddit Agent finds threads that rank on Google and new threads that are rising, and drafts a reply that you post yourself. Its AI Visibility scans check ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude.

Meelu is a desktop app for founders and marketers that covers the same jobs in one plan. It crawls your site, reads your Search Console and Analytics data, checks AI answers against your own pages, writes pages for your site, listens for conversations on nine platforms and the open web, and finds people worth a message.

## Where aiseo.ai is better

- **A Reddit workflow built for it.** It ranks threads by opportunity, suggests subreddits from your URL and sends daily alerts.
- **Writing tools.** An article generator, a humanizer and brand voices.
- **Integrations.** Search Console, Google Analytics, Ahrefs, Semrush and several content management systems.
- **A $1 trial** for a day on any plan.

## Where Meelu is better

- **One plan for all of it.** You do not buy SEO, AI visibility and writing separately.
- **More places to listen.** Your buyers also ask on LinkedIn, X, Hacker News and forums.
- **Leads.** The Find Leads desk finds people worth a personal message, with the reason for each.
- **It checks your page against each question**, not only whether AI mentions you.

## Pricing

aiseo.ai AI Visibility is $49 a month (Entry) or $149 a month (Pro), with 1 or 3 scans and Reddit credits a month. Writer plans are $24, $44 and $99 a month. The SEO Agent is a usage add-on from $49 a month. Every plan starts with a one-day trial for $1.

Meelu has no subscription and no tiers. Each of your first five websites costs 0.5 credits a day (1 credit is 1 US dollar), so about $15 a month, and each website after that costs 0.25 credits a day. AI model calls, keyword data, social listening and lead searches are charged at what they cost. Your first website is free for the first day.
