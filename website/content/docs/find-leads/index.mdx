The Find Leads desk finds people worth a personal message. You describe who you want to reach, in plain words. Meelu looks for them, drops the ones that are not a fit, and shows you the rest with the reason each one is worth your time and a link to their profile or public email.

You write the message and you send it yourself. Meelu never sends anything, never stores your messages and does not track replies.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Describe who you are looking for
    Write one sentence for each kind of person, such as "owners of small Shopify stores who say their Google traffic dropped". Each sentence is called a **brief**. Say who they are and what shows they need you. A website can have up to 10 briefs.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pick where to look
    Open a brief to choose its sources:
    - **Post authors**: people who recently posted about the problem on Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Facebook, Hacker News, GitHub or Dev.to.
    - **LinkedIn people**: profiles that match the roles in your brief.
    - **Web search**: websites and directories. Meelu shows an email only when the page itself prints it. It never guesses one.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Find leads
    Set **How many** leads you want, from 1 to 100, then click **Find leads**. Meelu turns your briefs into searches, collects people, and checks each one quickly. For the people who pass, it reads their recent posts and makes a final check. That is one round. If it has not found enough leads yet, it runs another round with new searches, up to 10 rounds. Each run brings people you have not seen before, and only posts from the last 30 days count.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Reach out yourself
    Each lead shows **Why** they are worth a message, the posts or pages behind it, and an **Opening angle**. Open their profile or email, write your own message, then click **Mark contacted**. If someone is not a fit, click **Discard**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Runs need the desktop app">
  Searches run from the Meelu desktop app. On the web you can edit your briefs, open profiles and mark people contacted or discarded. **Find leads**, **Suggest briefs from my website** and the **Ask Meelu** chat need the app.
</Callout>

## The Find Leads screen

From top to bottom you see:

- the header, with the **Ask Meelu** button (the ✱ icon), **How many** and **Find leads**,
- **Who you're looking for**, your briefs. Click the panel title to fold it away. Click a brief to open it and edit it in place, then click **Save and collapse**,
- the run progress while a run is going, or a line about the last run,
- the tabs **New**, **Contacted** and **Discarded**, each with a count,
- the leads for the tab you picked.

While a run is going, **Find leads** turns into **Stop run**. Stopping keeps the people already checked. People it had not reached yet can come up again in the next run.

## Credits

Each round costs about as much as a short run, so asking for many leads can take several rounds and cost more. A run only starts when your balance is at least $5, and only one run can go at a time on your account. Searches on Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Facebook, LinkedIn people search, reading recent posts, web search and the checks Meelu makes all use credits, at what they cost. See [Credits and billing](/docs/concepts/credits).

## How long leads are kept

- **New and contacted leads** stay until you discard them or delete the website.
- **Discarded leads** are deleted 30 days after you discard them. Meelu keeps only a short key, such as the profile handle, so the same person never comes back.
- **People Meelu decided were not a fit** are not shown again for 30 days. Meelu keeps only the same short key for them.

## Ask Meelu

In the desktop app, click the ✱ icon to chat about your leads. You can ask which leads to contact first, why a brief keeps finding the wrong people, or how to open a message to someone. Meelu suggests an opening; you write the message.

Meelu can also do a few things in this chat, once you agree:

- mark a lead contacted or discarded,
- add briefs, or suggest better wording for a brief,
- save a standing rule as a note, such as a kind of company to skip. See [Notes](/docs/concepts/notes).

It cannot start a run. Press **Find leads** for that.

## Meelu suggests, you decide

Every lead is a suggestion. Meelu tells you why the person surfaced and what they said. Whether to contact them, and what to say, is up to you. See [Suggestions](/docs/concepts/suggestions).
