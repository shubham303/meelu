Meelu is a desktop app with an AI coworker for your website. It reads your pages, looks at your Google Search Console and Google Analytics numbers, and helps you plan and write new pages. When it finds something worth your time, it tells you what it found and why it matters.

You run Meelu on your own computer, on macOS or Windows. You sign in with a Meelu account, add your website, and Meelu starts reading it.

## Meelu suggests, you decide

Meelu does not change your website on its own. It does not publish pages, rewrite your copy or post replies for you. Its job is to watch your site and your data, point at what it found, and explain the reason.

Every piece of automated work ends in a **suggestion**. A suggestion is a proposed change with its evidence: the data it came from, the pages involved and the reason. You can approve it, edit it or turn it down. When Meelu wants to save something, it asks you first.

You know your business, your customers and your taste. Meelu does the reading and the counting so you can make the call faster.
