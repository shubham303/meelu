The Analytics desk is a chat with an analyst. You ask a question about your website in plain words. Meelu looks at your data, runs the analysis and shows you what it found. Answers come with numbers, charts and the pages behind them, so you can check the answer yourself.

When an answer is worth keeping, you save it as a **report**. A report is a short, self-contained page with the finding, its charts and a note on where the numbers came from.

## What Meelu can look at

The Analytics desk reads your website's own data that is already in Meelu:

- **Search Console**: clicks, impressions, average position and click-through rate for your pages and search queries. Click-through rate (CTR) is the share of people who saw your page in Google and clicked it.
- **Google Analytics**: sessions, landing pages, traffic channels, countries, devices and events such as signups.
- **Your crawled pages**: the pages Meelu found on your site.
- **Your site audit**: the technical issues found on your pages.
- **Your content pieces**: the pages you wrote in the Content desk.

You do not upload any files. To get Search Console and Analytics data into Meelu, connect your Google account first. See [Connect Google](/docs/getting-started/connect-google).

## Where to find it

Click **Analytics** in the sidebar. While you are on the Analytics desk, the sidebar shows a short list under it:

- **New chat**: starts a fresh question. This shows only in the desktop app.
- Your recent chats, newest first. Click one to open it.
- **Show more**: loads older chats.
- **Reports**: opens the list of saved reports.

<Callout type="info" title="Chat in the desktop app">
  You can ask questions only in the Meelu desktop app. On the web, the Analytics page says **The analyst runs in the Meelu app**. You can still read your past chats and saved reports on the web.
</Callout>

## Meelu suggests, you decide

The Analytics desk only reads and explains. It does not change your pages, your content or your settings. When a finding points at something to fix, Meelu says what it suggests, shows the evidence and names the desk where you can act on it. What to do next is your call.
