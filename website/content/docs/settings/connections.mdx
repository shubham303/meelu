**Connections** is at the bottom of the sidebar. It holds the services Meelu reaches on your behalf. Everything you connect here is stored on this computer, encrypted, and never sent to Meelu's servers.

The screen has four cards, from top to bottom:

| Card | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Your Anthropic key** | Run tasks on your own Anthropic account instead of your Meelu credits. |
| **Default model** | Pick the AI model new tasks use. |
| **Google Search Console** | Your search numbers: impressions, clicks and positions for your own site. |
| **Google Analytics 4** | Your traffic: sessions, sources and key events for your pages. |

The first two cards are covered in [AI models](/docs/concepts/ai-models). This page covers the Google cards.

## Google Search Console

Search Console is the only measured search data Meelu has. It powers the SEO desk's **Search performance** screen and the index status of your pages.

Each Google card shows a status badge: **Connected** or **Not connected**. A connected card also says when you connected it.

## Google Analytics 4

Analytics shows what actually arrived on your site: sessions, channels, landing pages and key events. It powers the SEO desk's **Traffic** screen.

## Connect a Google service

<Steps>
<Step>

### Click Connect

Your browser opens Google's sign-in page. In Meelu, the button says **Waiting for Google…**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Sign in with the right account

Use the Google account that already has access to your site's property. Meelu asks for read-only access only.

</Step>
<Step>

### Go to the SEO desk to import

Once the card says **Connected**, open the SEO desk to pick your property and import your data. See [Connect Google Search Console and Analytics](/docs/getting-started/connect-google).

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Empty property list?">
If Meelu finds no properties after you connect, you signed in with a Google account that cannot see your site. Click **Reconnect** and pick another account. It does not mean your site has no data.
</Callout>

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** on the card, then confirm. Meelu deletes the Google sign-in from this computer. To remove Meelu's access on Google's side too, visit [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Data already imported stays with your website. Deleting the website removes it. See [Websites](/docs/settings/websites).

## Using another computer

Connections are stored on one computer. If you install Meelu on a second computer, connect Google there too.

## What each button does

| Button | Card | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Connect** | Google cards | Opens Google's sign-in to grant read-only access. |
| **Reconnect** | Google cards | Signs in again, for example with another Google account. |
| **Disconnect** | Google cards | Deletes the Google sign-in from this computer, after you confirm. |
| **Docs** | Google cards | Opens Google's own documentation for that service. |
| **Save key** | Your Anthropic key | Checks and saves your key. |
| **Remove key** | Your Anthropic key | Deletes your key from this computer. |
| **Claim credits** | Your Anthropic key | Opens Anthropic's page about monthly API credits for Max and Team plans. |
| **Use** | Default model | Makes that model the default for new tasks. |
| **Show all** (number) **models** / **Show fewer** | Default model | Expands or shortens the model list. |
