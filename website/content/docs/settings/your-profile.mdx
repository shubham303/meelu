---
title: Your profile
description: Tell Meelu who it writes as, so drafts sound like you.
---

Your **profile** is the **You** section on the Settings screen. It is about you as a person, not your business. It stays the same across all your websites.

Meelu uses it whenever it writes as you. If it is empty, content is written in no particular voice.

## The fields

| Field | What to put in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Your name** | Your own name. |
| **Role** | How you are introduced, for example "Founder" or "Head of Marketing". |
| **Company** | Your company's name. |
| **Timezone** | Your timezone as a place name, such as "America/New_York" or "Asia/Kolkata". Not an abbreviation like "EST" or "IST". |
| **Sign-off** | The exact lines you end a message with. Meelu uses them word for word, line breaks and all. |
| **How you want to come across** | Your voice, in your own words. For example: "Plain-spoken and direct. Short sentences, no jargon, never salesy." |
| **Short bio** | A line or two about you. Think of what a stranger would want to know before reading something you wrote. |
| **LinkedIn**, **X** | Links to your personal profiles. |
| **Your own site** | Your personal site, not the business site. |

Click **Save** when you are done. You see **Saved** next to the button.

<Callout type="idea" title="The voice field matters most">
**How you want to come across** has the biggest effect on drafts. Describe how you sound at your best, and what you never want to sound like. Words to avoid help as much as words to use.
</Callout>

## Let Meelu fill it in

If you would rather talk than type, click the sparkle button **Set this up with Meelu** in the top right of the **You** section. A task called **Set up your profile** opens. Pick a prompt chip, and Meelu asks you questions one at a time:

| Prompt chip | What Meelu does |
| --- | --- |
| **Set up my profile** | Interviews you about who you are, what you do and who you write for, then saves it all once you confirm. |
| **Set my sign-off** | Asks for your sign-off and reads it back so you can check the line breaks. |
| **Describe how I want to sound** | Asks how you sound at your best and what to avoid, then writes a short description for you to approve. |
| **Read back what you have** | Reads your profile back field by field and says which fields are still empty. It changes nothing unless you ask. |

Meelu asks before it saves anything. See [Approving what Meelu does](/docs/concepts/approving-actions).

## What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Set this up with Meelu** (sparkle) | Starts a task where Meelu interviews you and fills in your profile. |
| **Save** | Saves your changes. It is greyed out until you change something. |
