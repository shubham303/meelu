**Settings** holds the things that are about you and your copy of the app, not about one website. Open it from the sidebar, under **App**. It is also the only screen shown when you pick **Global** in the website switcher.

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **You** | Who Meelu writes as: your name, role, sign-off, voice and links. See [Your profile](/docs/settings/your-profile). |
| **Account** | The name and email you are signed in with, and a **Sign out** button. |
| **Model usage** | What AI model calls have cost since you opened Meelu this time. |
| **App updates** | Your app version and a button to check for a new one. See [App updates](/docs/settings/app-updates). |

## Account

Shows the name and email of the account you are signed in with. Click **Sign out** to sign out of Meelu on this computer. You can also sign out from the bottom of the sidebar.

To change your email or delete your account, contact Meelu at the address in the [Privacy Policy](https://meelu.tech/privacy).

## Model usage

Shows the cost of AI model calls in this sitting, meaning since you last opened Meelu. Under the amount, it shows the tokens sent and received and the number of turns. It is counted on this computer and starts again from zero each time you open the app.

Use it to get a feel for what your tasks cost. For a full history of charges, use the **Activity** tab on the **Credits** screen. See [Credits and billing](/docs/concepts/credits).

## Settings that live elsewhere

Some settings are kept next to the thing they control:

| Setting | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Add, switch or delete a website | The website switcher at the top of the sidebar. See [Websites](/docs/settings/websites). |
| A website's sitemap, target countries and language | The **The website** card on the Site desk. See [Website profile](/docs/site/website-profile). |
| Google Search Console and Google Analytics | **Connections**, at the bottom of the sidebar. See [Connections](/docs/settings/connections). |
| Your default AI model and your own Anthropic key | **Connections**. See [AI models](/docs/concepts/ai-models). |
| Saved prompts | **Your prompts**, on the SEO desk and on **Site**, **Internal links**. See [Your prompts](/docs/settings/your-prompts). |
| Rules and corrections for Meelu | The Notes desk. See [Notes](/docs/concepts/notes). |
| Balance and top-ups | **Credits**, at the bottom of the sidebar. See [Credits and billing](/docs/concepts/credits). |

## What each button does

| Button | Section | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Set this up with Meelu** (sparkle) | You | Starts a task where Meelu interviews you and fills in your profile. |
| **Save** | You | Saves changes to your profile. It is greyed out until you change something. |
| **Sign out** | Account | Signs you out of Meelu on this computer. |
| **Check for updates** | App updates | Checks for a new version now. |
| **Download** (version) | App updates | Appears when a new version is out. Opens the download in your browser. |
