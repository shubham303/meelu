---
title: Desks
description: What a desk is in Meelu, and what each desk is for.
---

A **desk** is a screen in Meelu for one kind of work. Each desk holds its own data and its own tasks. The Site desk knows your pages. The SEO desk knows your Google numbers. The Content desk holds the pages you are writing.

You find the desks in the sidebar, under **Website**. They all work on the website shown in the website switcher.

## The desks

| Desk | What it is for | Use it when you want to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | What is waiting on you, and what each desk holds. | Start your day and see what needs a look. |
| **Site** | What your pages actually say, how they link together and what is broken. | Find broken pages, orphan pages, missing links and technical problems. |
| **SEO** | How Google sees your site and what visitors do, from Search Console and Analytics. | See which searches bring people in, what dropped, and which keywords to go after. |
| **Content** | New pages for your website, with keyword, internal links, title, H1 and meta description, checked on every save. | Plan and write a page you will put on your site yourself. |
| **Social Listening** | Public conversations your site belongs in, found from what you describe. | Find posts where a reply from you would help, and draft that reply. |
| **Analytics** | Questions about your Search Console and Analytics data, answered with charts and evidence. | Ask "why" or "how much" questions, and keep the useful answers as reports. |
| **Notes** | What you have told Meelu once. It reads these before every task. | Write down a rule or a correction so you never repeat it. |

Under **App** there is one more screen, **Settings**, for things that are not about one website. See [Settings](/docs/settings).

## How desks work together

Each desk keeps to its own job. The Site desk does not rank keywords, and the SEO desk does not rewrite your pages.

Meelu, the agent, is what joins them up. When you ask a question that spans two desks, such as "which of my pages get impressions but have almost no internal links?", Meelu reads from both desks in the same task. You do not have to move the data yourself.

If a task needs something another desk owns, Meelu can bring that desk into the task on its own. For example, when you write a page on the Content desk, Meelu loads the Site and SEO desks to pick a target keyword and find pages to link from. Bringing a desk in only lets Meelu read more and suggest more. Anything it wants to save still needs your yes.

## Counting and charts live on the Analytics desk

Desks show their own data, but they do not answer open-ended number questions like "which section of my site has the most thin pages?" or "how did clicks trend by month?". Those belong on the [Analytics desk](/docs/analytics), which is built to count, compare and chart across all your data.
