---
title: Approving what Meelu does
description: How the permission prompt works, and what Allow, Always allow, Full access and No each mean.
---

Meelu can read your data freely. It can look at your pages, your Google numbers and your notes without asking. But whenever it wants to **save, change or delete** something in Meelu, it stops and asks you first.

Examples of things Meelu asks about:

- saving a content draft or a change to one
- saving a link suggestion or a report
- saving a note it offered to write down for you
- updating your profile after it interviewed you

Meelu never publishes to your website and never changes anything in your Google accounts. It posts on social media only a reply you approved, from an account you linked. Publishing and posting are always your call.

## The permission prompt

When Meelu wants to save something, a box appears in the task panel that starts with **Meelu wants to run**, followed by the name of the action. Under that, a short line shows the main details, such as the title of the page it wants to save.

Meelu waits until you answer. While it waits, the **Tasks** button in the corner says **needs you**, so you notice even if the panel is closed.

## What each button does

| Button | What it does | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Allow** | Lets this one action run. Meelu asks again next time. | The default. Use it while you are getting to know Meelu. |
| **Always allow this action** | Lets this kind of action run without asking again, until you quit the app. | When you trust a repeated step, such as saving each suggestion in a long review. |
| **Full access** | Lets every action run without asking, in every task, until you quit the app. | Only when you are watching closely and want Meelu to finish a long job without stopping. |
| **No** | Declines. Meelu does not try again. It says what it was going to do and stops. | When the action is wrong, or you want to change the request first. |

<Callout type="warn" title="Always allow and Full access reset when you quit">
Both last only until you quit Meelu. The next time you open the app, Meelu asks again. There is no way to turn them on permanently.
</Callout>

After you answer, the box shrinks to a single grey line that says whether the action was allowed or declined.

## Answers from the Tasks list

You can also answer from the **Tasks** list in the bottom-right corner. When something needs you, the list shows how many items need your yes, with a **Yes** and a **No** button on each. These work the same as **Allow** and **No**.

## Why Meelu asks

Meelu suggests, and you decide. Asking before every save keeps you in control of what ends up in your workspace. It also gives you a moment to check the details. If something looks off, click **No** and tell Meelu what to change. It will try again with your correction.
