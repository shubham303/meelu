**Notes** are the things you have told Meelu once, so you never have to say them twice. Meelu reads them before every task. A note outranks whatever Meelu was about to do.

Notes belong to one website. A note you write for one site does not apply to your other sites.

Open the Notes desk from the sidebar, under **Website**.

## Two kinds of note

| Kind | What it is for | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rule** | How you want things done. | "Never open a page with a rhetorical question." |
| **Correction** | Something Meelu got wrong once, so it does not happen again. | "We do not sell to agencies. Stop suggesting agency keywords." |

Both kinds work the same way. The label just helps you keep track of why each note exists.

## What makes a good note

A good test: is it still true next month? If yes, it is worth a note.

Good notes are:

- **specific**: "Our product is only sold in the UK and Ireland" beats "focus on our market"
- **short**: one idea per note, so you can edit or delete it on its own
- **in your words**: Meelu follows them as written

Do not use notes for one-off instructions. "Make this draft shorter" belongs in the task. "Keep blog intros under 60 words" belongs in a note.

## Find, edit and delete notes

- Use the **All**, **Rules** and **Corrections** filters to narrow the list. Each shows a count.
- Use the search box to find a note by its meaning or its words. For example, searching "pricing" can find a note that says "never mention what we charge".
- Hover over a note to see the edit and delete buttons.

## What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rule** / **Correction** | Sets the kind for the note you are writing or editing. |
| **Save note** | Saves a new note. |
| **All** / **Rules** / **Corrections** | Shows all notes, or only one kind. |
| Search box | Finds notes by meaning or by words. Press Enter to search. |
| **Clear search** (X) | Clears the search and shows every note again. |
| **Edit note** (pencil) | Opens the note so you can change its text or kind. |
| **Save** | Saves your edit. |
| **Cancel** | Closes the edit without saving. |
| **Delete note** (bin) | Deletes the note. Meelu asks you to confirm. This cannot be undone. |

<Callout type="info" title="Notes and your profile are different">
Notes are about one website and how to work on it. Your profile, in **Settings**, is about you: your name, your sign-off and your voice. It is shared across all your websites. See [Your profile](/docs/settings/your-profile).
</Callout>
