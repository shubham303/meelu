Meelu is a desktop app. You install it on your computer, sign in, and do all your work there. The agent, the crawl and your Google connections all run on your own machine.

<Callout type="warn" title="Meelu is in beta">
Meelu is updated often, and you may run into bugs. The app is not yet verified by Apple, Microsoft or Google, so you will see a security warning when you download and open it. This is expected. Only download Meelu from [meelu.tech/download](https://meelu.tech/download).
</Callout>

## Which download to pick

Go to [meelu.tech/download](https://meelu.tech/download). The page picks the right build for your computer, but you can choose another one.

| Download | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Download for macOS** (Apple Silicon) | Macs with an M1, M2, M3 or M4 chip |
| **Download for macOS** (Intel) | Older Macs with an Intel processor |
| **Download for Windows** | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

Not sure which Mac you have? Open the Apple menu and choose **About This Mac**. If it lists a "Chip" starting with "Apple", pick Apple Silicon. If it lists a "Processor" from Intel, pick Intel.

When you download, the site may ask for your email address and country so it can tell you about fixes and new versions. You can skip this.

## Open it for the first time

Each warning below appears only once. After that, Meelu opens normally.

<Tabs items={["macOS", "Windows", "Chrome and Edge"]}>
<Tab value="macOS">

macOS says: *"Meelu" Not Opened. Apple could not verify it is free of malware.*

1. Open the downloaded file and drag Meelu into **Applications**.
2. Open Meelu once. When macOS blocks it, click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**. Scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Meelu.
4. Confirm with your password.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Windows">

Windows says: *Windows protected your PC. Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting.*

1. Run the installer you downloaded.
2. When the blue SmartScreen window appears, click **More info**.
3. Check that the app is Meelu, then click **Run anyway**.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Chrome and Edge">

Your browser may say the file *isn't commonly downloaded*, or mark the download as unverified.

1. Open the browser's downloads list.
2. Choose **Keep**. In Chrome it is under the three-dot menu. In Edge it is under the three-dot menu, then **Show more**.
3. If the browser asks again, confirm that you trust the file.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Why there is no web version

Meelu only runs in the desktop app. If you open your Meelu address in a web browser, it sends you to the download page. The app needs to be on your computer because it:

- runs the agent that reads, plans and drafts for you
- crawls your website from your own machine
- keeps your Google sign-in on your device, not on Meelu's servers

Read more in [Why Meelu is a desktop app](/docs/concepts/desktop-app).
