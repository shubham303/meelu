title: Getting started
description: Go from download to a first look at your website in Meelu in about fifteen minutes.
---

This section takes you through setup in order. Each step builds on the one before it, so it is best to follow them in sequence the first time.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Install the desktop app

Download Meelu for macOS or Windows and get past the first-launch security warning. See [Install the app](/docs/getting-started/install).

</Step>
<Step>

### Create your account

Sign up with your email, confirm the 6-digit code and tell Meelu who it writes as. See [Create your account](/docs/getting-started/create-account).

</Step>
<Step>

### Add your website

Type your domain and business name. Meelu starts writing up what your site is about. See [Add your first website](/docs/getting-started/add-a-website).

</Step>
<Step>

### Run your first crawl

Meelu reads every page your sitemap lists. Every desk is built on what the crawl reads. See [Run your first crawl](/docs/getting-started/first-crawl).

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect Google

Connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics so Meelu works from your real numbers. See [Connect Google Search Console and Analytics](/docs/getting-started/connect-google).

</Step>
<Step>

### Find your way around

Learn the sidebar, the website switcher, the Overview desk and the task panel where you talk to Meelu. See [A tour of the dashboard](/docs/getting-started/tour).

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="idea" title="Try it before you install">
You can click through a copy of the dashboard filled with sample data at [meelu.tech/demo](https://meelu.tech/demo). Nothing you do there touches a real account.
</Callout>

## What it costs to get started

Your first website is free for the first three days of your account. Crawling your site and importing your Google data cost nothing. Some work, like AI model calls and keyword data, uses credits. See [Credits and billing](/docs/concepts/credits) for the details.
