A **crawl** is when Meelu visits your website and reads its pages one by one, the way a search engine does. Until the first crawl finishes, most desks have nothing to show. The crawl runs from your own computer and uses no credits.

## Start the crawl

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Site desk

Click **Site** in the sidebar. Right after you add a website, Meelu opens this desk for you. It says **This website has not been read yet**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Click Crawl website

The button is at the top right of the Site desk, and also in the middle of the empty screen.

</Step>
<Step>

### Keep Meelu open while it runs

A progress bar shows what the crawl is doing. You can use other desks while it runs, but keep the app open. A small site takes a few minutes. A site with thousands of pages takes longer.

</Step>
<Step>

### Look at what it found

When the crawl ends, the Site desk shows four numbers: **Sitemap coverage**, **Broken**, **Not in sitemap** and **Orphans**. The **Pages**, **Internal links** and **Technical SEO** sections fill in too.

## What the progress bar says

| Message | What is happening |
| --- | --- |
| **Reading the sitemap and robots.txt** | Meelu reads your sitemap (the file that lists your pages for search engines) and your robots.txt (the file that tells crawlers what they may read). |
| **Reading the pages the sitemap lists** | Meelu reads every page your sitemap lists. |
| **Following links the sitemap did not list** | Meelu follows links on your pages to find pages the sitemap missed. |
| **Stopping — the pages already read are kept** | You clicked **Stop crawl**. Nothing already read is lost. |

## If something goes wrong

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Meelu says no sitemap was found">
A box appears under the crawl button. Paste the full address of your sitemap, such as `https://example.com/sitemap.xml`, and click **Use this sitemap**. Meelu remembers it for later crawls. Not sure where your sitemap is? Most website builders put it at `/sitemap.xml` or `/sitemap_index.xml`.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Most pages come back blocked">
Your firewall or bot protection may be treating the crawl as a bot. The Site desk gives you a **Crawl access token** you can allow in your firewall. See [If your firewall blocks the crawl](/docs/site/crawling#if-your-firewall-blocks-the-crawl).
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## After the first crawl

While the app is open, Meelu crawls each of your websites again about once a day, so the desks stay current. Click **Crawl again** yourself after you change your site, for example after you publish a page or fix broken links.

For everything about crawling, including limits and pages Meelu skips, see [Crawling your site](/docs/site/crawling).

## What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Crawl website** | Starts the first crawl of this website. |
| **Crawl again** | Starts a new crawl of a website that has been crawled before. |
| **Stop crawl** | Stops the crawl and keeps the pages already read. |
| **Use this sitemap** | Crawls using the sitemap address you pasted, and remembers it. |
