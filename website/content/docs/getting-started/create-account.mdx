When you open Meelu for the first time, you see the sign-in screen. If you are new, create an account from there.

## Sign up

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the sign-up screen

On the **Welcome back** screen, click **Create an account**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in your details

Enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters. Your name is optional here. Click **Create account**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm your email

Meelu emails you a 6-digit code. Type it in and click **Confirm email**. The code expires after 10 minutes. If it does not arrive, wait a minute and click **Send a new code**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Tell Meelu who it writes as

The next screen asks **Who should Meelu write as?** Only your name is needed now. Click **Open my dashboard** to finish.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The "Who should Meelu write as?" screen

This screen is about you, not your business. It stays the same however many websites you add. Meelu uses it to sound like you when it drafts.

| Field | What to put in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Your name** | Your own name. This is the only required field. |
| **Role** | How you are introduced, for example "Founder". |
| **Timezone** | Your timezone as a place name, such as "Europe/London" or "Asia/Kolkata", not an abbreviation like "GMT". Meelu fills it in from your computer when it can. |
| **How you sign off** | The exact lines you end a message with. Line breaks are kept. |
| **How you want to come across** | Your voice in your own words, for example "Plain-spoken and direct. Short sentences, no jargon, never salesy." |

You can fill in or change all of this later in **Settings**. In the app you can also ask Meelu to interview you and fill it in. See [Your profile](/docs/settings/your-profile).

<Callout type="idea" title="Spend a minute on your voice">
If **How you want to come across** is empty, Meelu writes in no particular voice. Two or three sentences here make drafts sound more like you.
</Callout>

## Signing in later

On the **Welcome back** screen, enter your email and password and click **Sign in**.

If you never confirmed your email, Meelu asks for the code before letting you in.

## Forgot your password

<Steps>
<Step>

### Ask for a code

On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot password?**. Enter your email.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set a new password

If an account exists for that email, Meelu sends a 6-digit code. Enter it with your new password and click **Set new password**. The code expires after 10 minutes.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Create an account** | Opens the sign-up form. |
| **Create account** | Creates your account and emails you a confirmation code. |
| **Confirm email** | Checks the 6-digit code and signs you in. |
| **Send a new code** | Sends a fresh code. It is greyed out for 60 seconds after each send. |
| **Use a different email** | Goes back to the sign-up form so you can fix the email address. |
| **Open my dashboard** | Saves your profile and opens Meelu. |
| **Sign in** | Signs you in with your email and password. |
| **Forgot password?** | Starts the password reset. |
| **Set new password** | Saves your new password and signs you in. |
