A **backlink** is a link from another website to yours. Links from relevant, trusted sites help your pages rank. The easiest sites to win a link from are often those that already link to your competitors. The **Backlink opportunities** screen lists them.

Open it from the **Backlink opportunities** card on the SEO home screen.

## Where the list comes from

Click **Fetch backlinks** next to a [competitor](/docs/seo/competitors) to pull its backlinks, and yours the first time. Only the competitors you fetch count. A site is an opportunity when it links to at least one competitor and never links to you. Sites that link to the most competitors come first.

Not every backlink is worth chasing. Meelu leaves out links that:

- **Look spammy**: a high spam score for the link or the linking site, a page with hundreds of outbound links, or a site on a bare IP address.
- **Look paid**: links marked sponsored, pages about sponsored or guest posts, and links repeated across a whole site.
- **Are too weak to help**: sites with a very low domain rank, dead pages, and redirects instead of real links.

## What each column and button shows

| Column or button | What it shows or does |
| --- | --- |
| **Site** | The linking site, with a link to the page that links to a competitor. |
| **Links to** | Which of your competitors it links to. |
| **Domain rank** | How strong the linking site is, out of 1000. |
| **Spam** | The spam score of the linking site, out of 100. Lower is better. |
| **Link** | Whether the link passes ranking value (follow) or not (nofollow). |
| **Dismiss** | Hides the site from the list for good, even after backlinks are fetched again. |
| **Previous** / **Next** | Move through the list, 25 sites at a time. |
| **Ask Meelu** (sparkle icon) | Opens a task about backlinks. Desktop app only. |
| Task cards under **Start a task** | Ready-made tasks, listed below. Desktop app only. |

| Task card | What Meelu does |
| --- | --- |
| **Why do they link to competitors?** | For the top sites, finds what they link to on your competitors' sites and why, so your request fits. |

## Tips

- Run **Why do they link to competitors?** before you reach out. A request that matches why a site already links (a resource list, a review, a comparison) is far more likely to work.
- Fetching a competitor's backlinks uses credits, so fetch the rivals you care about most. See [Credits](/docs/concepts/credits).
