The **Traffic** screen shows what visitors do on your site. The numbers come from your Google Analytics (GA4) property. Search performance tells you how people find you in Google. Traffic tells you where all your visitors come from and whether they do what you want.

Open it from the **Traffic** card on the SEO home screen.

## Before you start: connect and sync

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Connect Google Analytics
    If Analytics is not connected, the screen shows **Google Analytics is not connected**. Click **Connect Google Analytics** and sign in with a Google account that can read this site's property. See [Connect Google](/docs/getting-started/connect-google).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Import your history
    Click **Import 16 months of traffic data**. A progress bar shows **Importing traffic data — day X of Y**. Click **Stop** if you need to.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pick the property
    Analytics does not say which website a property measures, so Meelu asks **Which Analytics property measures this site?** Pick the right one from the list. The sync then continues.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Keep it up to date
    Click **Sync now** later to bring in the newest days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Free, and runs on your computer">
Syncing does not use credits. It runs in the desktop app, and the Google connection stays on your device. The web dashboard can show the data but cannot start a sync.
</Callout>

### Time range

Choose **28 days**, **90 days** or **180 days**. Next to it, a line shows how many sessions came from **Organic Search**, which means unpaid Google and other search engine results.

### Metric tiles

| Tile | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sessions** | Visits to your site. One person can make several sessions. |
| **Users** | Distinct visitors. |
| **Key events** | The actions you marked as important in Analytics, such as a sign-up or a purchase. Google used to call these conversions. |
| **Revenue** | Money recorded by Analytics, if your site sends it. |

Each tile shows the change against the previous range. Click a tile to show it in the chart. When **Sessions** is chosen, the chart shows two lines: all sessions and Organic Search sessions.

### Channels

**Channels · last N days** shows where sessions come from, such as Organic Search, Direct, Referral or Paid Search.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Channel** | The traffic source group. |
| **Sessions** | Visits from this channel. |
| **Δ** | Change against the previous range. |
| **Share** | This channel's part of all sessions. |
| **Engaged** | The share of sessions where the visitor really engaged, as Analytics defines it. |
| **Key events** | Important actions from this channel. |
| **Revenue** | Revenue from this channel. |

### Landing pages

**Landing pages · last N days** lists the first page of each visit. Use **All channels** to show only one channel, for example only Organic Search. Type in **Filter pages…** to find a page. Click **Sessions**, **Δ**, **Key events** or **Revenue** to sort.

This is where you find pages that get traffic but no key events, and pages that convert well but get few visits.

### Pages viewed

**Pages viewed · last N days** lists every page people looked at, not only the first one. Columns are **Views**, **Δ**, **Users**, **Avg. time** (average engaged time per user) and **Key events**.

### Breakdown

**Breakdown · last N days** splits your traffic in other ways. Pick a tab:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sources** | Each source and medium, such as google / organic. |
| **Events** | Each event Analytics recorded, with its count. |
| **Countries** | Where visitors are. |
| **Devices** | Desktop, mobile or tablet. |
| **Systems** | Operating systems. |
| **Browsers** | Browsers. |

A note at the bottom names the Analytics property in use.

## What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect Google Analytics** | Connects your Google account in the desktop app. |
| **Import 16 months of traffic data** | Runs the first sync. Shown only when there is no data yet. |
| **Sync now** | Brings in the newest days. |
| **Stop** | Stops a running sync. |
| **28 days** / **90 days** / **180 days** | Sets the time range for the whole screen. |
| Metric tiles | Choose what the chart shows. |
| **All channels** menu | Filters Landing pages to one channel. |
| **Filter pages…** | Finds pages by address. |
| Column names | Sort the table. |
| Breakdown tabs | Switch the breakdown table. |
| **‹ Prev** / **Next ›** | Page through a table. |
| **Ask Meelu** (sparkle icon) | Opens a task about your traffic. Desktop app only. |

## Questions to ask Meelu here

The **Ask Meelu** button on this screen offers four prompts:

- **Where does my traffic come from?** Which channels and sources carry the site, and how the mix moved.
- **What actually converts?** Which landing pages and channels produce key events, not just visits.
- **What changed and why?** The biggest movements, and whether each is a real shift or a one-day spike.
- **Is organic pulling its weight?** The share Organic Search carries, how engaged those visitors are, and whether it is growing.

See [Asking the SEO desk](/docs/seo/asking-the-seo-desk).
