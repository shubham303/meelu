Every SEO screen lets you hand a question to Meelu. Meelu reads your Search Console and Analytics data, your clusters and the search market, then answers with the evidence. These features work in the desktop app. See [Working with the agent](/docs/concepts/working-with-the-agent).

## Three ways to start

| Where | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask Meelu** (sparkle icon, top right of each screen) | Opens an empty task with suggested prompts for that screen. Type your own question or pick a suggestion. |
| Task cards under **Start a task** | Start a ready-made task with one click. Meelu begins at once. |
| Cards under **Your prompts** | Your own saved prompts for this website. Click one to run it. |

A task opens in the task panel. You can answer Meelu's questions, approve what it proposes, or tell it to change course. When Meelu wants to save something, such as competitors, it shows you first and saves only what you agree with.

## Task cards on the SEO home screen

| Task card | What Meelu does |
| --- | --- |
| **Long-tail keywords from People Also Ask** | You give a keyword. Meelu turns Google's People Also Ask questions and autocomplete suggestions into long-tail keywords, groups them, checks their volume and ranks them by intent, buyers first. |
| **Keyword ideas from a seed** | You give a seed keyword or topic. Meelu finds related keywords, groups them, drops the groups that miss the intent, and tells you how many are informational, commercial and transactional. |
| **Content to match a competitor** | You name a competitor. Meelu finds keywords it ranks for and you do not, picks 10 groups, and for each suggests editing one of your pages or writing a new one, plus which pages should link to it. |
| **Plan content for a topic** | You give a topic. Meelu first asks what the content is for, what value it should give readers, and whether you want one page or a series. Then it plans a hub page and supporting pages from keyword clusters, says which of your pages to update and which to add, and puts it in a report. It does not write the content. |
| **Striking distance keywords** | Lists searches where you rank at position 8 or lower with more than 10 impressions and few or no clicks, with the page that ranks for each. |
| **Build clusters for a topic** | You name a topic. Meelu proposes seed keywords, waits for your OK, then builds clusters and reports what it dropped. |
| **Cluster my Search Console queries** | Builds clusters from the searches you already appear for. |

The other screens have their own task cards. See [Keyword clusters](/docs/seo/keyword-clusters), [Tracked keywords](/docs/seo/tracked-keywords), [Competitors](/docs/seo/competitors) and [Backlink opportunities](/docs/seo/backlinks).

## Suggested prompts on Search performance

The **Ask Meelu** button on [Search performance](/docs/seo/search-performance) offers:

- **Striking distance keywords**
- **Why did this page drop?** You name a page. Meelu compares its searches with the previous period, checks whether it is still indexed, and says what the data can and cannot explain.
- **Where am I invisible?** Which pages cannot perform at all because Google does not hold them.

The [Traffic](/docs/seo/traffic) screen has its own four prompts.

## Saving your own prompts

If you ask the same thing often, save it.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create the card
    Under **Your prompts** on the SEO home screen, click **New prompt**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Fill it in
    Give it a **Name**, which shows on the card. Write the **Prompt**, which is sent to Meelu exactly as written when you click the card.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save prompt**. The card is saved for this website only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Hover a saved card to see two icons: **Edit** opens the prompt so you can change it and click **Save changes**, and **Delete** removes it after you confirm.

## What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask Meelu** | Opens a new task for the screen you are on, with suggested prompts. |
| A task card | Starts that task straight away. |
| **New prompt** | Opens **Save a prompt** to create your own card. |
| **Save prompt** / **Save changes** | Saves a new prompt or your edits. |
| **Cancel** | Closes the editor without saving. |
| **Edit** (pencil icon on a saved card) | Opens the prompt for editing. |
| **Delete** (bin icon on a saved card) | Deletes the saved prompt after you confirm. |

## Getting good answers

- **Ask for evidence.** Meelu's SEO prompts ask it to use measured numbers and to say plainly when the data is too thin. You can ask the same in your own prompts.
- **Name the page or keyword** when your question is about one thing.
- **Expect suggestions, not changes.** Meelu tells you what it found and why it matters. You decide what to do with your site. See [Suggestions](/docs/concepts/suggestions).
- Tasks use credits. See [Credits](/docs/concepts/credits).
