---
title: Competitors
description: Keep a list of the domains that compete with you in search, so Meelu can compare and find gaps.
---

Your search competitors are not always your business competitors. A search competitor is any domain that chases **the same searchers for the same thing**. The **Competitors** screen holds your saved list.

The list matters because other parts of the desk use it:

- [Keyword clusters](/docs/seo/keyword-clusters) show which saved competitors rank for each cluster.
- **Find competitor keyword gaps** builds clusters from keywords two or more competitors rank for and you do not.
- [Backlink opportunities](/docs/seo/backlinks) are found from your competitors' links.

Open it from the **Competitors** card on the SEO home screen.

  <Tab value="Ask Meelu">
    Click the **Find my competitors** task card. Meelu looks across your markets for domains that win the searches you want. It leaves out marketplaces, encyclopedias, forums and news sites, because they share keywords with everyone. It shows you the list with a reason for each domain, and saves only the ones you agree with.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Add by hand">
    Type a domain, such as `competitor.com`, in the box under **Saved competitors** and click **Add**. You can add several at once, separated by spaces or commas.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The list holds up to 500 domains. The count shows under the title.

## What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Add** | Saves the domains typed in the box. |
| **Open site** | Opens the competitor's website in a new tab. |
| **Remove** | Removes the domain from the list right away. |
| **Ask Meelu** (sparkle icon) | Opens a task about your competitors. Desktop app only. |
| Task cards under **Start a task** | Ready-made tasks, listed below. Desktop app only. |

| Task card | What Meelu does |
| --- | --- |
| **Find my competitors** | Finds who wins the searches you want, with a reason for each, and saves the ones you approve. |
| **Clean up this list** | Points out saved domains that only share words with you, such as marketplaces, forums and news sites. Removes them once you agree. |
| **Compare me with a competitor** | You name one competitor. Meelu shows the searches where it outranks you, which of its pages win them, and what those pages do that yours do not. |

## Tips

- Keep the list focused. Five to fifteen real rivals give better gaps and backlink ideas than fifty loose matches.
- Removing a domain is instant and has no confirmation step. You can add it back at any time.
