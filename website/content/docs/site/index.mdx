The Site desk is where Meelu reads your website and tells you what it found. It answers three questions:

- **What do my pages say?** Every page Meelu has read, and the text inside it.
- **How are my pages connected?** Which pages link to which, and which pages are hard to reach.
- **What is technically wrong?** Problems that stop search engines and AI tools from reading your site well.

Everything on this desk starts with a **crawl**. A crawl is when Meelu visits your site and reads its pages, the same way a search engine does. Until you crawl, the desk has nothing to show.

<Callout type="info" title="Meelu suggests, you decide">
The Site desk never changes your website. It reads your pages, points at what it found and explains why it matters. You make any change on your site yourself, in your own website editor.
</Callout>

### The four numbers

After the first crawl, four cards sum up the health of your site.

| Card | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Sitemap coverage** | How many of the pages your sitemap lists Meelu could read. A **sitemap** is a file on your site that lists the pages you want search engines to find. Below 100% means some listed pages did not load. |
| **Broken** | Pages that returned "not found" (404) or "gone" (410). The hint below the number also counts pages that blocked Meelu, had a server error or did not answer. |
| **Not in sitemap** | Pages Meelu found by following links, but your sitemap does not list. Either add them to the sitemap or decide they should not be in search. |
| **Orphans** | Pages in your sitemap that no other page on your site links to. Visitors and search engines struggle to find them. |

If your sitemap lists pages that no longer exist, a yellow warning appears above the cards. Search engines see dead entries in a sitemap as a sign of a poorly kept site, so it is worth cleaning up.

### The three sections

Below the numbers are three cards. Click one to open that screen.

| Section | What you find there | Badge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pages** | Every page the crawl has read, and the passages inside them. | How many pages are on record. |
| **Structure** | How pages are reached from the home page. | How many pages are hard to reach, or "all reachable". |
| **Technical SEO** | Every check Meelu can answer from the pages it has read. | How many checks are open, and how many you turned down. |

At the bottom of the screen is **The website** card. See [The website record](/docs/site/website-profile).

## What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Crawl website** | Reads your site for the first time. Shows as **Crawl again** after the first crawl. Works in the Meelu desktop app only. |
| **Stop crawl** | Shows while a crawl runs. Stops it and keeps the pages already read. |
| **Ask Meelu** (sparkle icon) | Opens a chat with Meelu about this site. Desktop app only. See [Asking the Site desk](/docs/site/asking-the-site-desk). |
| **Use this sitemap** | Shows only when no sitemap was found. Paste your sitemap's address and Meelu crawls with it and remembers it. |

## How the Site desk relates to other desks

The Site desk is the base the other desks build on. A **desk** is one area of Meelu with its own screens and its own job. See [Desks](/docs/concepts/desks).

- **SEO desk.** The Site desk knows what your pages say. The SEO desk knows how Google sees them, from your Search Console and Analytics data. Some Site desk ideas, such as pillar pages for shared search queries, use that data too.
- **Content desk.** When Meelu writes a new page for you, it reads what the Site desk knows to pick the internal links for that page.
- **Analytics desk.** Counting, averaging and comparing across many pages is the Analytics desk's job. When you ask a question like "which section has the most thin pages?", Meelu can bring in that desk.

<Callout type="idea" title="Desktop app or browser?">
You can look at the Site desk in the browser or in the Meelu desktop app. They look the same. Crawling, rendering and asking Meelu only work in the desktop app, because they run on your own computer.
</Callout>
