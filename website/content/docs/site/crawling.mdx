A **crawl** is when Meelu visits your website and reads its pages, one by one, like a search engine does. Every screen on the Site desk is built from what the crawl read. If the crawl is old, the screens are old too.

<Callout type="info" title="Crawling runs in the desktop app">
The crawl runs in the Meelu desktop app, from your own computer. In the browser, the crawl button is greyed out. Keep the app open while a crawl runs. A crawl uses no credits.
</Callout>

Open the Meelu desktop app and click **Site** in the sidebar.

<Step>
### Click Crawl website

Click **Crawl website** at the top right. If the site has been crawled before, the button says **Crawl again**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Watch the progress bar

A progress bar shows which step the crawl is on and how many pages it has read. You can keep working in other desks while it runs.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read the result

When the crawl ends, a short note says how many pages were read. The four numbers on the Site screen and the Pages, Structure and Technical SEO screens update.
</Step>

## What the crawl reads

The crawl starts from your **sitemap**. A sitemap is a file on your site, often at `/sitemap.xml`, that lists the pages you want search engines to find. If you have not given Meelu a sitemap, it looks in the usual places for one.

The crawl works in three steps. The progress bar names the step it is on:

| Step shown | What is happening |
| --- | --- |
| **Reading the sitemap and robots.txt** | Meelu reads your sitemap and your `robots.txt`. **robots.txt** is a file that tells crawlers which pages they may read. |
| **Reading the pages the sitemap lists** | Meelu reads every page in your sitemap. |
| **Following links the sitemap did not list** | Meelu follows links on your pages to find pages your sitemap left out. |

Some limits to know:

- **Up to 10,000 pages per crawl.** If your sitemap lists more, the crawl stops there and does not follow links. A note tells you so.
- **Link-following is capped.** The crawl reads at most twice as many extra pages as your sitemap lists, so extra pages never crowd out the ones you listed.
- **Only your own site.** Links to other websites are recorded but not read.
- **Pages robots.txt blocks for Google are not read.** Meelu records them, because Google will not read them either.

### Pages Meelu skips

Some pages are read but marked as not worth studying. They show a grey badge in the Pages table:

| Badge | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **legal** | Privacy, terms, cookie or refund pages. |
| **pagination** | Page 2, 3 and so on of a list. |
| **thin** | Fewer than 80 words of text. |
| **redirect** | The address sends the visitor to another page. |

## If no sitemap is found

If Meelu cannot find a sitemap, a box appears under the crawl button. Paste your sitemap's full address, such as `https://example.com/sitemap.xml`, and click **Use this sitemap**. Meelu crawls with it and remembers it for later crawls. You can also add sitemaps in [the website record](/docs/site/website-profile).

## If your firewall blocks the crawl

The crawl reads your site from your computer, so a firewall or bot protection may treat it as a bot and block it. To let it through, use the **Crawl access token** in [the website record](/docs/site/website-profile#crawl-access-token).

## Stopping a crawl

Click **Stop crawl** while a crawl runs. The button shows **Stopping…** for a moment. Meelu keeps every page it has already read, so a stopped crawl is not wasted.

## Daily crawls

While the desktop app is open, Meelu crawls each of your sites again about once a day. It only does this for a site you have crawled at least once, and it crawls one site at a time. When a crawl started this way, the progress bar says **started on its daily schedule**.

If the app is closed, no daily crawl happens. The next one starts soon after you open the app.

<Callout type="idea" title="When to crawl by hand">
Click **Crawl again** after you change your site, such as after you publish a new page, fix broken links or change your menu. Then the Structure and Technical SEO screens show the new state, and suggestions you already acted on can be cleared.
</Callout>

## What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Crawl website** / **Crawl again** | Starts a crawl of this site. Desktop app only. |
| **Stop crawl** | Stops the running crawl and keeps what it read. |
| **Use this sitemap** | Crawls with the sitemap address you pasted and remembers it. |
