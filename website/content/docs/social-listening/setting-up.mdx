title: Set up listening

All the setup lives in the listening card at the top of the Social Listening screen. It has two rows: **Listening for**, with your lines, and **On**, with your platforms.

## Lines

A **line** is one plain sentence that describes a kind of conversation you want to find. Write it the way you would tell a colleague. Say who is posting and what they want.

Good lines look like this:

- "people asking which tool tracks their Google rankings"
- "small sites that lost traffic after a Google update"
- "freelancers asking how to chase a late invoice"

You do not write keywords. Meelu turns each line into search terms on its own, keeps posts that are close in meaning, and then reads each post to check it fits.

To keep an eye on a brand or a competitor, write a line for it too, such as "people talking about Ledgerly" or "teams looking for a cheaper FreshBooks alternative".

A website can have up to 30 lines.

### Add your first lines

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open the desk
    Click **Social Listening** in the sidebar. With no lines yet, the card opens ready for editing.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Describe a conversation
    Type a sentence in the box that says **Describe a conversation to listen for**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add it
    Press Enter or click **Add**. The line appears in the list, turned on.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Repeat
    Add a few more lines. Three to six well-written lines are a good start.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The line list

When you edit your lines, each one shows in a table:

- **On**: a switch. Lines that are off are not searched, but they stay in the list.
- **Line**: your sentence.
- **Kept**: how many posts from this line passed every check and reached your tabs.
- **Found**: how many posts the search found for this line before checking.

A line with a high **Found** and a low **Kept** usually finds the wrong posts. Reword it to say more clearly who is posting and what they want.

When you are not editing, the card shows up to three of your lines that are on, each with its **Kept** count, and a **+N more** chip for the rest.

### What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit lines** | Opens the full list of lines for editing. |
| **+N more** | Opens the full list, the same as **Edit lines**. |
| **Find a line** | Filters the list as you type. It shows when you have more than five lines. |
| **Collapse** / **Show less** | Closes the list and goes back to the short view. |
| The **On** switch | Turns a line on or off. |
| **Remove line** (the X) | Deletes the line. |
| **Add** | Adds the sentence you typed as a new line. It is greyed out when you already have 30 lines. |

To change a line's wording, remove it and add the new wording.

## Platforms

The **On** row shows every platform as a chip. A highlighted chip is on. Click a chip to turn it on or off. A run searches every platform that is on.

| Platform | Credits per result |
| --- | --- |
| Hacker News | No |
| Stack Exchange | No |
| Dev.to | No |
| GitHub Issues | No |
| Reddit | Yes |
| X (Twitter) | Yes |
| LinkedIn | Yes |
| Bluesky | Yes |
| Facebook | Yes |
| Web search | Per search, not per result |

**Web search** looks past the platforms above. For each line, AI searches the open web for recent forum threads, Q&A pages and community posts where someone raises what the line describes, and skips the platforms that are on. It leaves out articles and other pages you cannot reply on. Its finds then go through the same checks as every other post.

The card reminds you which platforms use credits per result. A greyed-out chip is not available right now and cannot be turned on.

On the web, the card says **Pick platforms in the Meelu app**. You choose platforms in the desktop app.

<Callout type="idea" title="Let Meelu help with lines">
  In the desktop app, click **Ask Meelu** and ask for better lines. Meelu reads your site and your past results and suggests new or sharper wording. It adds lines only after you agree.
</Callout>
