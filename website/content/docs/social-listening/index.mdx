title: Social Listening desk
description: Find conversations on social platforms where a reply from you would help, and decide which ones to answer.
---

The Social Listening desk finds posts where a reply from you belongs. You describe the kinds of conversations you want to join, in plain words. Meelu searches social platforms for matching posts, keeps the ones that fit, and says why each one surfaced.

You decide which posts to answer. You write the reply, with help from Meelu if you want it, and you post it yourself. If you linked a social account on the **Connections** screen, Meelu can post a reply you approved instead. It never posts on its own.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Describe what to listen for
    Write one sentence for each kind of conversation, such as "people asking which tool tracks their Google rankings". Each sentence is called a **line**. Pick the platforms to search. See [Set up listening](/docs/social-listening/setting-up).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Run a search
    Click **Run now**. Meelu turns your lines into search terms, searches the platforms, and reads each post to check that it really fits what you wrote. See [Runs](/docs/social-listening/runs).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Decide what to answer
    The posts that fit land in **To reply**. Each shows why it surfaced. Draft a reply, skip the post, or mark it not relevant. See [Posts and replies](/docs/social-listening/posts-and-replies).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Post it yourself
    Copy your reply, open the post on the platform and paste it there. Then mark the post replied in Meelu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Platforms

You can listen on these platforms:

- Hacker News
- Stack Exchange
- Dev.to
- GitHub Issues
- Reddit
- X (Twitter)
- LinkedIn
- Bluesky
- Facebook
- Web search: forum threads, Q&A pages and community posts anywhere else on the web

Reddit, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Bluesky and Facebook use credits for each result they return. Web search uses credits for each search it runs. See [Runs](/docs/social-listening/runs#credits).

<Callout type="info" title="Runs need the desktop app">
  Searches run on your computer, in the Meelu desktop app. On the web you can see your lines, read the posts, change their status and write replies by hand. Running a search, picking platforms, asking Meelu to draft a reply and the **Ask Meelu** chat need the app.
</Callout>

### What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Download CSV** | Downloads every post Meelu is keeping for this website as a spreadsheet file. While it builds, it reads **Building CSV…**. |
| **Run now** | Starts a search with your lines and platforms. See [Runs](/docs/social-listening/runs). |
| **Stop** | Shows in place of **Run now** while a search is going. Stops it. Posts already checked stay. |
| **Ask Meelu** (the sparkle icon) | Opens a chat about this desk. Desktop app only. |

## Ask Meelu

In the desktop app, click the sparkle icon to chat with Meelu about your posts. You can ask things like which posts to reply to first, why a line keeps finding nothing, or how to approach a post.

Meelu can also do a few things in this chat, once you agree:

- suggest a reply angle for a post, which then shows in its reply panel,
- move a post between **To reply**, **Drafted** and **Done**,
- add new lines or suggest better wording for a line that keeps finding the wrong posts,
- save a standing rule as a note, such as a forum to avoid or a competitor never to name. See [Notes](/docs/concepts/notes).

It does not write your replies for you in this chat, and it cannot start a search. Press **Run now** for that.

## Meelu suggests, you decide

Every post Meelu finds is a suggestion. It tells you why the post surfaced and how a reply might fit. Whether to reply, and what to say, is up to you. See [Suggestions](/docs/concepts/suggestions).
