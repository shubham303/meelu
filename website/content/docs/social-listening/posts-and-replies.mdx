The posts Meelu keeps are sorted into three tabs. Each tab shows its count.

| Tab | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **To reply** | New posts that fit your lines and are waiting for your decision. |
| **Drafted** | Posts where you copied a reply but have not yet marked it replied. |
| **Done** | Posts you replied to, skipped or marked not relevant. |

The list shows 25 posts at a time. Click **Show N more** at the bottom to load more.

## The post card

Each post shows as a card.

- **The top line**: the platform, then the subreddit (such as r/freelance) or the author's handle, how long ago it was posted, and its score and number of replies where the platform has them.
- **The line that found it**, on the right, in **To reply** and **Drafted**. In **Done**, a badge shows the outcome instead: **Replied**, **Skipped** or **Not relevant**.
- **The title and text** of the post. Click **show more** to read a long post in full.
- **Why it surfaced**, in **To reply**: a short reason why this post fits what you wrote.
- **Your draft**, in **Drafted**: the reply you copied, and when you copied it.

### What each button does

In **To reply**:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open post** | Opens the post on its platform in a new tab. |
| **Not relevant** | The post does not match what you listen for. It moves to **Done**, marked **Not relevant**. Use this for a wrong match. |
| **Skip** | The post fits, but you do not want to answer it. It moves to **Done**, marked **Skipped**. |
| **Mark done** | You already replied some other way. It moves to **Done**, marked **Replied**. |
| **Draft reply** | Opens the reply panel for this post. |

In **Drafted**:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open post** | Opens the post on its platform. |
| **Edit draft** | Opens the reply panel with your saved draft. |
| **Mark replied** | You posted the reply. It moves to **Done**, marked **Replied**. |

In **Done**:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open post** | Opens the post on its platform. |
| **Move back to To reply** | Puts the post back in **To reply** if you change your mind. |

- the post itself,
- an **Angle**, when Meelu has one: two or three sentences on how a reply from you could fit the thread, what to lead with and what not to pitch,
- **Your reply**: a box where you write.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Write your reply
    Type your reply in **Your reply**. Use the angle as a guide if it helps.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Ask Meelu to help, if you want
    In the desktop app, type an instruction in the box under your reply, such as "draft it", "make it shorter" or "less salesy", and press Enter. Meelu rewrites the reply in the box. Repeat until you like it, or edit it yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Copy it
    Click **Copy reply**. Meelu copies the text, saves it as your draft, moves the post to **Drafted** and closes the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Post it yourself
    Click **Open post**, paste your reply on the platform and post it there.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Mark it replied
    Back in Meelu, click **Mark replied** on the post in **Drafted**. It moves to **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When Meelu helps with a reply, it answers the person's question first. It mentions your site only where it really helps, once, and without a link unless you ask for one. It matches the platform: short and plain on X, conversational on Reddit and Hacker News, with no hashtags, no emoji and no marketing tone. It keeps what you wrote unless you ask it to change it. It reads your business profile and your notes to match how you write.

### What each button does

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy reply** | Copies your reply to the clipboard, saves it as a draft, moves the post to **Drafted** and closes the panel. It is greyed out while the reply is empty. |
| **Open post** | Opens the post on its platform in a new tab. |
| **Mark replied** | Saves your reply text and moves the post straight to **Done**, marked **Replied**. Use it when you have already posted. |
| The **Ask Meelu** box | Sends your instruction to Meelu, which rewrites the reply in the box. Desktop app only. |

If your computer blocks copying, Meelu says so. Select the text in the box and copy it yourself.

<Callout type="warn" title="Meelu never posts on its own">
  Meelu does not like or start posts. It posts a reply only when you approve it, to a thread you picked, from a Reddit, LinkedIn or GitHub account you linked on the **Connections** screen. Without a linked account, you post every reply yourself.
</Callout>

## Keep your lines sharp

How you treat posts tells you how good your lines are. If one line's posts keep ending up as **Not relevant**, reword it to say more clearly who is posting and what they want. In the desktop app you can ask Meelu to suggest better wording. See [Set up listening](/docs/social-listening/setting-up).
