---
title: How long posts are kept
description: Older posts disappear from the desk after 30 days, so download a copy of anything you want to keep.
---

Conversations on social platforms move fast. A thread from last month rarely needs a new reply. So Meelu keeps posts for a limited time.

## Posts older than 30 days disappear

Meelu removes a post 30 days after it was posted on its platform. If the platform did not say when it was posted, Meelu counts from the day it found the post.

This applies to every tab: **To reply**, **Drafted** and **Done**. A draft you saved on an old post goes away with the post.

Your lines, platforms and settings are not removed. Only posts are.

## Posts you have seen do not come back

Once Meelu has shown you a post, a later run does not show it again. This is true even if you skipped it or marked it not relevant. If you want to answer a post you skipped, open **Done** and click **Move back to To reply**, while it is still there.

## Keep a copy

To keep a record, click **Download CSV** in the header before posts age out. You get a spreadsheet file, `social-listening-posts.csv`, with every post Meelu is keeping for this website. Each row has:

| Column | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `url` | The link to the post. |
| `platform` | The platform it is on. |
| `status` | Where it is: to reply, drafted, replied, skipped or rejected (not relevant). |
| `title` | The post title, if it has one. |
| `text` | The post text. |
| `authorHandle` | The author's handle. |
| `postedAt` | When it was posted. |
| `line` | The line that found it. |
| `similarity` | How close in meaning the post was to the line. |
| `keepReason` | Why it surfaced. |
| `suggestedAngle` | The suggested reply angle. |
| `replyText` | Your saved reply. |

**Download CSV** is greyed out until you have at least one line.
