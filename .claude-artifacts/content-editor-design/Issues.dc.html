Warehouse jobs in Nashik: what they pay in 2026
412 words·2 min read·edited 3 min ago

Most listings for warehouse work in Nashik never mention the number that matters. They say "competitive" and leave it there. Here is what the roles actually pay, based on the 140 openings we tracked between January and August.

Picker and packer

Entry-level picking pays ₹13,500 to ₹16,000 a month at the Ambad and Sinnar clusters, with a ₹1,500 attendance bonus common at the larger 3PL sites. Night shifts add 12 to 15 percent.

Forklift operator

A licence moves the range to ₹19,000 to ₹24,000. Reach-truck certification is the single biggest jump we saw — it is scarce enough that two sites were paying above the band to fill it.

Shift supervisor

Supervisors start around ₹28,000 and top out near ₹38,000 with a WMS background. Almost every posting asked for two years on the floor first.
